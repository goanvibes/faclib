Library Activities

Certificate Course, short-term library science course, Knowledge Bazar and Full Circle.

Original pageAll files

The library conducts a Certificate Course in Library & Information Science approved by the Directorate of Higher Education, Government of Goa.

The short-term course lets students complete library work hours and learn basic library skills.

Knowledge Bazar is a week-long programme of exhibitions, reference resources, videos and career guidance. Full Circle is the library newsletter.
