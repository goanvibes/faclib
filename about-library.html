About Library

Profile of Fr. Agnel College Library, its holdings, digital resources, best practices and training role.

Original pageAll files

Fr. Agnel College Library is one of the finest college libraries in Goa. It is computerized, open access and bar-coded, with a collection that includes books, journals, bound volumes, audio-video CDs, maps, project works, photographs and other resources.

Readers can access e-journals, e-books and e-databases through INFLIBNET N-LIST and National Digital Library of India. The library website provides question papers, curriculum, project works, presentations, notes and subject portals.

Best practices include additional library cards, the Library Credit Programme, Knowledge Bazaar, certificate courses in Library and Information Science, computerization work and training for budding librarians.
